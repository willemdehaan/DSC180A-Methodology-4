## Quarter 2 Project Proposal Brainstorm

**1. What is the most interesting topic covered in your domain this quarter?**

The idea of auditing model fairness through sequential hypothesis testing, in which evidence against the null hypothesis accumulates over time until a predefined threshold is crossed, leading to the rejection of the null hypothesis of model fairness.

**2. Describe a potential investigation you would like to pursue for your Quarter 2 Project.**

Constructing a novel definition of model fairness to be applied to models being trained in the healthcare and insurance industries.

**3. What is a potential change you’d make to the approach taken in your current Quarter 1 Project?**

Using more datasets to apply a range of different techniques developed in the paper we are replicating.

**4. What other techniques would you be interested in using in your project?**

Although not necessary for my research topic, being able to scrape data and construct a dataset would be a technique I am interested in using during this project.
